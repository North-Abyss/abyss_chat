# Abyss Chat v1.1.5 Release Notes

We are thrilled to announce the release of **Abyss Chat v1.1.5**, bringing a massive overhaul to media viewing, powerful new in-chat search traversal, and crucial bug fixes for group calls!

## 🌟 What's New & Improved

### Immersive Media Experience
- **Full-Screen Media Player:** Say goodbye to placeholders! You can now watch videos and listen to audio directly inside the full-screen `MediaViewerScreen`.
- **Beautiful Audio Cards:** When listening to music in full screen, you'll be greeted by a stunning 5:4 ratio audio player card featuring a floating music note, playback controls, and duration tracking.
- **Advanced Video Controls:** The full-screen video player now comes equipped with a comprehensive control bar including a volume slider, mute toggle, progress scrubber, and play/pause controls.
- **Smart Carousel Icons:** The bottom media carousel in the viewer now intelligently displays intuitive icons (Video 🎥, Music 🎵, Document 📄) for non-image files instead of broken thumbnails.

### Search & Navigation
- **Contextual Chat Search:** We've revamped the chat search! Instead of filtering your messages and losing context, the search bar now features up/down arrows that allow you to traverse and jump between matching messages directly within the chat history.

### Logic & Bug Fixes
- **Accurate Group Call Banners:** Fixed a bug where Active Group Call banners would sometimes appear in P2P chats instead of the group chat they belonged to.
- **Quick End Call:** You can now quickly hang up or cut a call using the new "End" button directly from the Active Call Banner in the chat list.
- **Draw Match History:** When a mini-game ends in a draw, the result is now accurately saved to the chat history so you have a record of the attempt!

---
**Thank you for using Abyss Chat!** As always, your conversations remain completely decentralized, encrypted, and serverless.
